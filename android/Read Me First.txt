CueComm for Android

1. Copy CueComm.apk to the phone and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from that app: allow it.
2. Open CueComm. Allow the Microphone and Notifications.
3. To join a session: tap the menu (three lines, top left), tap the session box, then either
   - tap a session found on your network, or
   - type the IP address of any device that is hosting (for example 203.0.113.5:47800) under "Join by IP
     address" and press Join. You do not need to type the session name.
4. Press Listen to speak into a channel. Say a cue word to send OSC and flash every device in the session.

Works with CueComm for Mac in the same session. Over the internet, forward TCP port 47800 on the hosting
network's router, or put the devices on a VPN such as Tailscale. Connections are not encrypted.
